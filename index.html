04 — Train Like a Teammate

Academy Standards

Tap each card to learn the basics of being a safe, respectful training partner.

🧼 Hygiene
Arrive clean and train in freshly washed gear. Wash your uniform after every training session.
🦶 Footwear
Keep outdoor footwear off the mats and follow academy expectations when moving between mat areas and other spaces.
💍 Jewellery
Remove jewellery and anything else that could injure you or your training partner.
🤝 The Tap
Tap early when you need to. When your partner taps, release immediately.
🛡️ Safety
Technique and control come before winning a training round.
🤒 Illness
If you're sick or have a contagious skin concern, stay off the mats and follow academy guidance.
💬 Communication
Tell your partner or coach if something hurts or if you need to adjust the intensity.
🧠 Attitude
Be coachable. Ask questions. Help your teammates. Leave your ego at the door.

06 — Choose Your Path

How Do You Want to Train?

Hayabusa offers multiple disciplines. Tap each one for a quick introduction.

🥋 Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu
Grappling using the traditional Gi. You'll explore Positional control, escapes, sweeps, transitions and submissions. What to wear Approved team Gi and belt.
⚡ No-Gi / Grappling
Submission grappling without the traditional Gi. You'll explore Control, movement, wrestling, escapes and submissions. What to wear Team rashguard and shorts.
🤼 Wrestling
Develop takedowns, positioning, pressure and control. You'll explore Entries, finishes, scrambling and mat control.
🥋 Judo
A throwing and grappling art built around balance, timing and technique. You'll explore Gripping, throws, takedowns and ground control.
🥊 Kickboxing
Develop striking fundamentals, movement and conditioning. You'll explore Punches, kicks, combinations, footwork and defence.
🥊 MMA
Mixed Martial Arts brings striking and grappling together. You'll explore Transitions between striking, takedowns and ground work.

11 — Questions Are Normal

New Member FAQ

Tap a question for the answer.

What should I wear?
Wear the approved uniform for your class. If you're unsure, ask before training.
Do I need to be fit before I start?
No. Training itself helps build fitness. Start at your own pace.
Do I need martial arts experience?
No. Beginners learn the fundamentals and build from there.
What if I don't understand something?
Ask. Questions are part of learning.
How often should I train?
A sustainable, consistent routine is more useful than doing too much too quickly.
When will I get promoted?
Promotion reflects development over time and is determined by the coaching team.
Do I have to compete?
No. Competition is optional.
Where do I buy team gear?
Use the Team Store section of this onboarding experience.
What if I'm injured?
Tell your coach and communicate with your training partners.
Who should I ask if I'm unsure?
Talk to your coach or academy staff. You are not expected to know everything when you start.
